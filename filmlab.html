Pitch reference

Origin = left corner flag on the near touchline; X runs goal-to-goal, Y across the pitch. Box geometry (penalty areas, six-yard boxes, penalty spots, centre circle) is fixed by the Laws of the Game, so those presets are exact on any pitch. Only corner flags and halfway-line points depend on the length/width above — set them (club sites, league handbooks, or measure the pitch on Google Earth) before using those presets. Unsure of the dimensions? Calibrate from box geometry only and everything stays accurate.
